Fillet weld

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculation has not been validated for design decisions.

A fillet weld checked by the simplified method (force per unit length) or the directional method (stresses on the throat). Everything runs in this browser.

Checked

  • Throat thickness and effective length (EN 1993-1-8 4.5.2)
  • Simplified method (4.5.3.3) or directional method (4.5.3.2), βw per Table 4.1
  • Long welds in lap joints flagged with βLw,1 (4.11)

Not checked

  • Distribution of a load over a weld group, eccentricity
  • Penetration, fatigue, the parent material and execution
