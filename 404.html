Connection checks

EA Suys

Calculation on request

EA Suys checks steel, timber and masonry connections with its own programs: Eurocode design checks with drawings, and a finite-element check where the code method needs one. The checks are available on request; they no longer run on this site.

Send us the connection (a sketch or the drawings), the member sizes, the materials and the design forces. You receive the check with its drawings.

Ask for a check

Steel (EN 1993-1-8, EN 1992-4)

  • Bolted T-stub in tension: failure modes 1 to 3 with prying, with a finite-element cross-check.
  • Column base with cast-in anchors, pinned or fixed: compression, uplift and shear, the anchors in concrete and the column weld.
  • Bolted beam-to-column end plate, end-plate beam splice and double-sided connection to a beam web: rows and groups, moment resistance and stiffness.
  • Bolted lap joint, bolt group under eccentric load, fillet weld and plate in tension.

Timber (EN 1995-1-1)

  • Steel-to-timber and timber-to-timber dowel-type connections, screws and toothed plates.
  • Timber contact moment joints, proprietary brackets with a European Technical Assessment, and timber bearing.

Existing masonry

  • Fixings in existing masonry.
