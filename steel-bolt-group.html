Bolt group under eccentric load

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculation has not been validated for design decisions.

A rectangular bolt group loaded in its plane by shear forces and a moment about its centroid (an eccentric shear load). The bolt forces follow from the elastic polar-moment method; each bolt is checked in shear and in bearing for both force components. An elastic FE model of the plate tests the rigid-plate assumption. Everything runs in this browser.

Checked

  • Bolt forces by the elastic method (V/n + T r / Σr²)
  • Shear and bearing of every bolt, components verified separately (EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.4, note 3); detailing per Table 3.3 (warnings)
  • Elastic FE of the plate on bolt springs against the polar method

Not checked

  • Plastic (instantaneous centre) distribution, slip-resistant bolts, bolts in tension, prying
  • The supporting member, the plate's own resistance, non-rectangular groups

EN 1993-1-8:2005 and EN 1993-1-1:2005 with the recommended partial factors (no National Annex). What the tools have been compared with, and the limits: Validation and scope.
