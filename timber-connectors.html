Screws and toothed plates

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculations have not been validated for design decisions.

Check axially loaded screws for withdrawal, head pull-through and screw tension, or check a single-shear toothed-plate connection including its connecting bolts. The bolt check uses the effective number from EN 1995-1-1 (8.34)–(8.35) and the capped rope effect when a characteristic axial resistance is known.

Checked

  • Axially loaded screws, EN 1995-1-1:2004+A1:2008 8.7.2
  • Toothed plates C1–C9 and C10–C11, plus bolt Johansen modes, EN 1995-1-1 8.10 and 8.5
  • Recommended EN partial factors; verify the National Annex that applies

Not checked

  • Screw group spacing and splitting; axial/lateral interaction
  • Toothed-plate row group effect, washer bearing, splitting, members away from the joint
  • No finite element analysis is used or needed for these clause based checks
