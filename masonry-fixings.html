Fixings in existing masonry

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculations have not been validated for design decisions.

Check grouted dowels or pins in shear, bed-joint sliding, dead-weight uplift ties and pocket hold-downs in solid existing masonry. The routes rely on bearing and stabilising weight only; they do not rely on resin bond or masonry tension.

Checked

  • Grouted dowel bearing modes and rod shear, including sliding of the surrounding units
  • Bed-joint shear along or across the wall using EN 1996-1-1 3.6.2
  • Dead-weight uplift tie and pocket hold-down, with favourable weight per EN 1990 EQU
  • Masonry gamma_M from Belgian NBN EN 1996-1-1 Table 2.1-ANB, by category and execution class S/N; it may be raised, never lowered

Not checked

  • Resin bond, masonry tension, hollow or unbonded masonry, or drilling into bed joints
  • Wall stability, local splitting, reinforcement, and anchor group interaction beyond the listed routes
  • No finite element analysis is used or needed for these formula checks
