Bolted beam-to-column end plate

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculation has not been validated for design decisions.

Moment-resisting connection of a beam to the flange of a continuous, unstiffened I/H column. Set the beam, column, end plate, welds, bolt rows and design actions. The check updates as you edit the inputs.

Checked

  • Column flange and end-plate T-stubs, bolt tension and beam web tension, individually and in row groups (EN 1993-1-8 Tables 6.2, 6.4 and 6.6; Figure 6.11)
  • Effective row resistances in sequence, compression-side limits and triangular distribution (6.2.7.2); column web panel, column web and beam compression; stiffness (6.3)
  • Two bolts per row; plate and weld detailing checks in the scope of the route
  • FE check of the end-plate tension zone in this browser: end plate with all tension rows, beam web and tension flange as shells with plasticity, bolt springs and contact on the column flange (rigid), at γ = 1 against the EN component model — a check of the model, not a design value

Not checked

  • Stiffeners, backing plates, a nearby column end, bolt shear, weld resistance or member design; shear and axial actions are not combined with moment
  • For a double-sided joint, moments of opposite sign need a separate column web panel check
  • Column web panel and compression side: EN model only. Optional FE includes the column flange and a column-web support strip with its far edge fixed.

FE verification of the tension zone (in this browser)

Shell model of the end plate with all tension bolt rows, the beam web and the tension flange, with plasticity, bolt springs and contact on the column flange. By default the column flange is rigid; the option below includes its bending and a supported column-web strip in the same FE model. The beam end turns about the centre of compression step by step to the limit load or an earlier bolt capacity event. FE resistance = limit load where the force-displacement curve flattens (twice-elastic-slope rule); 5 % strain is shown for information. It uses characteristic strengths (γ = 1) and is compared with the EN component model at γ = 1 — a check of the component model, not a design value.

Drag to rotate. End plate, beam web and tension flange at the resistance event; bolt forces in kN (red: bolt at 0.9 fub As).
