Proprietary brackets and hangers

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculations have not been validated for design decisions.

Check Simpson Strong-Tie ABR105 and ABR90 using encoded tables from ETA-06/0106, or the SBE80/150 hanger using cited product data. Non-ETA fastener layouts are refused. Rigid-support checks are encoded for ABR105 only.

Checked

  • ABR105 Annex D3 and ABR90 Annex D1 beam-to-beam capacities and interactions
  • ABR105 rigid-support tables and exact encoded fastener patterns
  • SBE80/150 directional resistance, with combined loading flagged when its interaction is not encoded
  • Source citations and encoded values are listed below

Not checked

  • Unlisted nail, anchor, bracket or fastener arrangements; service class 3
  • Rigid-support checks for ABR90; unspecified ETA values are refused
  • SBE timber-to-concrete anchorage and combined vertical/lateral interaction
  • No finite element analysis is used or needed for these product-table checks
