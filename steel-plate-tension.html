Plate in tension

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculation has not been validated for design decisions.

A rectangular steel plate in tension with holes in one critical section across its width. Everything runs in this browser.

Checked

  • Gross section Npl,Rd (EN 1993-1-1 6.2.3 (6.6))
  • Net section Nu,Rd = 0.9 Anet fu / γM2 (6.7)
  • fy, fu by thickness (Table 3.1)

Not checked

  • Staggered holes, shear lag, block tearing (see the lap joint), eccentric tension
  • Category C connections (Nnet,Rd), the bolts
