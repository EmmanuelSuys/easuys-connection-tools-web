Bolted T-stub in tension

Feasibility prototype — not for design use. The calculation has not been validated for design decisions.

The T-stub is the basic component of bolted end plates, column flanges and base plates in tension. Enter the geometry, steel grade and bolts: the drawing and the EN 1993-1-8 design check update as you type. The finite-element verification runs in this browser — the input stays on your device.

Checked

  • EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2 modes 1–3 with prying, for an equal-leg T-stub with one bolt row on each side of the web
  • Web in tension (6.2.6.8), bolt punching (Table 3.4), spacing and edge distances (Table 3.3)
  • FE shell model with plasticity, contact and bolt springs, as a cross-check at γ = 1

Not checked

  • The effective length: enter L = leff of the governing yield-line pattern yourself
  • Welds and the root fillet (enter the EN m), bolt shear and bearing, preload, bolt bending
  • Bolt rows acting as a group, backing plates, the connected members
